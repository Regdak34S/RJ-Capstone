### 5. Past-tense question
**Question:** What would have made your past content-creation process easier or helped you spend less time organizing and more time actually creating?
**Answer:** He said having one place that showed his ideas, steps, and progress all together would have saved him a lot of time.

## Existing workaround
Before this project, Mike used a mix of phone notes, scattered folders, and quick checklists to handle his content-creation process. This included saving clips wherever he had space, writing ideas in whatever app was open, and tracking progress mentally. The workaround helped keep things barely functional, but it was limited because nothing connected together, and he constantly wasted time searching for files or figuring out what step came next.
